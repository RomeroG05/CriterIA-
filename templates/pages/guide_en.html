User guide

CriterIA helps you understand and analyze ethical dilemmas about artificial intelligence, communication and journalism. It answers only from the PDF documents in its corpus.

1. Start a conversation

  1. Choose a mode: Concepts, Cases or Dilemmas.
  2. Type your question in Spanish, English or Portuguese. The assistant detects the language and replies in it.
  3. Press Enter to send and Shift + Enter for a new line.

2. What each mode does

  • Concepts: defines a term or principle and explains its key points.
  • Cases: presents cases described in the documents, with a summary and the ethical issue.
  • Dilemmas: identifies the principles in tension, the alternatives and their consequences. It does not decide for you: it gives you elements to decide.

3. Check the sources

Each statement ends with a number, such as 2. Clicking it opens the exact PDF page with the passage highlighted. From there you can open the document or generate the citation in APA 7, Chicago, MLA, BibTeX or RIS.

4. When it cannot answer

  • Ambiguous question: it will ask for more details.
  • Outside its scope: it will remind you which topics it covers.
  • No evidence in the PDFs: it will say so rather than make up an answer. Try rephrasing.
  • System error: you will see a notice and a Retry button.

5. Voice

Press the microphone, speak and press it again to finish. The text appears in the box so you can review it before sending. Transcription runs on the project's server.

6. Account and history

You can use the chat as a guest, but the conversation is not saved. With an account you get history (search, rename, delete), PDF export, answer feedback and image and audio generation.

7. Image and audio

Under each grounded answer you can generate an illustration or listen to it as audio. These are created with Gemini from the text of the answer; the content always comes from the PDFs.

8. A recommendation

The assistant can be wrong. Always check the source before using an answer in your work or in an editorial decision.
